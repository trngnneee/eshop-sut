# Participant Information and Consent

## Study

HW03 Task 2 — Moderated usability evaluation of EShop's account flow.

## What will happen

You will be asked to use a test website to prepare an account and profile while thinking aloud. You will then complete ten rating questions and four short interview questions. The session takes approximately 20–25 minutes.

## Important points

- We are testing the product, not you.
- Participation is voluntary.
- You may pause, skip a question, or stop at any time without penalty.
- Do not enter your real password, phone number, home address, or other private information into EShop. Use only the test data provided.
- Screen recording is required for an official study session.
- Audio recording is optional and requires separate consent.
- The submitted report uses a participant ID and masked contact. Unmasked contact is kept separately only for possible teaching-assistant verification.
- A short genuine quote may be included under your participant ID, never under your name.
