## 5. Conclusion — When should AI be used (or not)?

Write 80–150 words describing patterns you observed. Where did AI shine? Where did AI fail? What is your recommendation for using AI in this kind of work in the future?

Instead of relying on AI to generate test cases directly, I used Claude to streamline the testing workflow. Claude was first used to analyze the UI inventory and extract it into a structured draft checklist. I then manually reviewed the checklist, identified missing scenarios and edge cases that the AI did not capture, and added these gaps before executing the tests manually.

During the testing process, Claude also assisted with formatting issue reports and preparing them for submission to GitHub. While this significantly reduced the time spent on documentation and organization, the quality of the final results still depended on manual validation and domain knowledge. AI proved to be an effective productivity tool for generating drafts and improving consistency, but human judgment remained essential for identifying missing test scenarios, verifying defects, and ensuring the final deliverables met the assignment requirements.
